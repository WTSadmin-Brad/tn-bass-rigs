## Cross-source synthesis (2026-04-27)

Bullets are organized by dossier topic. Each bullet cites the source file path; the dossier translates these into inline citations with full source names.

### Components — convergent findings

- **Mainline.** Modern default is 10–15 lb braid (Sufix NanoBraid, Seaguar Smackdown, Berkley X9 / FireLine the named brands across cross-sponsorship sources). Trend has moved heavier from the 8-lb-braid 2015–2019 norm. Power/cover variant ("Bubba Shot") drops braid for straight 12–15 lb fluorocarbon on a baitcaster. Cite: `tacklewarehouse-drop-shot-gear-guide.md`, `wired2fish-palaniuk-a-list-2022.md`, `mlf-wheeler-table-rock-stage-seven-2019.md`, `wired2fish-meyer-bubba-shot-2021.md`.
- **Leader.** 6–12 lb fluorocarbon, **8–12" baseline length** (Iaconelli 10–12"; Martens 8–10"; Wheeler shorter for cover). Condition-driven length: 6" cruising / spawn / cold; 20–24"+ deep clear or tall cover; 3–4 ft for height-keyed suspended fish. `mikeiaconelli-blog-mastering-dropshot-2024.md`, `mlf-martens-drop-shot-101-2019.md`, `bassresource-palaniuk-rojas-cruising-2016.md`, `bassniper-drop-shot-length-weight-bait-2020-03.md`, `bassfishinglife-drop-shot-leader-length-2025-05.md`. Premium leader fluoro (Seaguar Gold Label / Tatsu) earns its place where abrasion matters (cover, structure); `wired2fish-palaniuk-a-list-2022.md`.
- **Hook — three legitimate orientation lanes:**
  - Nose-hook open-finesse default: #1–#2 in VMC RedLine/Drop Shot, Gamakatsu G-Finesse, or Berkley Fusion19. St. Lawrence 2024 dominance (7-of-10 top-10) shows this convergence across sponsorships. `bassmaster-top-lures-st-lawrence-2024.md`, `wired2fish-palaniuk-a-list-2022.md`.
  - Texas-rigged threaded for cover: VMC Finesse Neko 1/0–#2 (Wheeler 2019 win, Palaniuk Setup 2, DeFoe southern) or Owner Cover Shot 1/0–2/0 (Meyer Bubba Shot, Gore Wheeler 2024, Sands Arkansas River 2026). `mlf-wheeler-table-rock-stage-seven-2019.md`, `wired2fish-defoe-southern-drop-shot-2024.md`, `wired2fish-meyer-bubba-shot-2021.md`, `bassmaster-top-lures-wheeler-2024.md`.
  - Wacky-rigged for vertical/FFS spotted-bass / pressured fish: Owner Mosquito (Meyer 2019). `[THIN]` — single named source. `wired2fish-meyer-wacky-drop-shot-2019.md`. Empirical confirmation of vertical use case in `bassniper-drop-shot-length-weight-bait-2020-03.md`.
  - Outlier: Gamakatsu Light Wire Rebarb 1/0 Texas-style on stained TVA water (Pirch Chickamauga 2022 9th). `bassmaster-top-lures-chickamauga-2022.md`. `[STALE]` but corroborated by Wheeler-2024 1/0-Texas-rigged pattern.
- **Weight.** 3/16-oz to 1/2-oz tungsten; **1/4-oz is the most-cited single number** across cached sources. Material universally tungsten in modern (2019+) sources. Shape splits four ways with condition reasoning — cylinder (cover/timber, Wheeler), teardrop (best feel / no hangups, Palaniuk), round (mud-bottom anchor / cruising), pencil/skinny (Tackle Warehouse standard). Brands appearing across tournament data: VMC Tungsten Teardrop, VMC Cylinder, Strike King Tour Grade, SPRO, WOO! Tungsten, Eco Pro. Family-level "1/4-oz tungsten cylinder or teardrop" is the safe default; brand-level [SPONSOR-FLAG] sub-bullets earned. `tacklewarehouse-drop-shot-gear-guide.md`, `mlf-wheeler-table-rock-stage-seven-2019.md`, `wired2fish-palaniuk-a-list-2022.md`, `bassmaster-top-lures-wheeler-2024.md`, `bassmaster-top-lures-st-lawrence-2024.md`. Empirical: heavier weight adds erratic recoil action — don't assume "lightest you can get away with"; match weight to fish mood (`bassniper-drop-shot-length-weight-bait-2020-03.md`).
- **Terminal hardware.** Not used. Drop-shot is a clean three-component rig (line → hook → leader → weight). Worth dossier-noting absence explicitly. No source in the cache shows beads, swivels, or clips on a tournament-standard drop-shot.
- **Knots — `[CONTESTED]` at the hook.** Tackle Warehouse + most pros: Palomar (with tag end re-routed back through hook eye to keep hook standing perpendicular). Aaron Martens explicitly **rejects** Palomar in favor of **Double Uni** at the hook for hookset reliability. `tacklewarehouse-drop-shot-gear-guide.md` vs. `mlf-martens-drop-shot-101-2019.md`. For braid-mainline-to-fluoro-leader: Alberto knot (Lucas) or quadruple overhand / 4-wrap surgeon's (Reese) — `bassmaster-drop-shot-tips-from-pros-2015.md` `[STALE]`. Weight pinches on tag end (slot-style tungsten — no knot). Cody Meyer "Bubba Shot" Palomar-with-tag-through-eye reportedly uses the same eye-rerouting trick; `wired2fish-meyer-bubba-shot-2021.md`.
- **Rod.** Two distinct profiles by application. (a) Open-water finesse default: 6'10"–7'3" medium-light to medium fast-tip spinning (Tackle Warehouse, Palaniuk 6'10" Alpha Angler DSR, Meyer 7'1" Daiwa Tatula, DeFoe 7'1" southern). (b) Cover / heavy-line variant: 7'2" medium-heavy spinning (Wheeler) or 7' medium-heavy casting (Meyer Bubba Shot). Reese 2015 parabolic preference is `[CONTESTED]` `[STALE]` and minority.
- **Reel.** 2500–3000-size spinning (Daiwa Exist 3000, 4000 for line-twist mitigation per Tackle Warehouse). Bubba Shot variant: 6.3:1 baitcaster.

### Top-3 baits — convergent findings

- **Universal default — 4–5" salt-impregnated straight-tail finesse worm** in green pumpkin / morning dawn / "Aaron's magic" / prism shad. Roboworm and X-Zone Slammer earn brand-level mention via cross-sponsorship convergence (Roboworm: Gore 2024, Palaniuk cruising 2016, multiple St. Lawrence top-10; X-Zone Slammer: Palaniuk Setup 1, multiple St. Lawrence top-10). `bassmaster-top-lures-st-lawrence-2024.md`, `bassmaster-top-lures-wheeler-2024.md`, `wired2fish-palaniuk-a-list-2022.md`, `bassresource-palaniuk-rojas-cruising-2016.md`.
- **Highland-smallmouth FFS-vertical — 3–4" shad-shape minnow imitation.** Berkley MaxScent Flat Worm, SPRO Sakura, X-Zone, Strike King KVD Finesse converge across St. Lawrence 2024 top-10 and TN-River-system smallmouth callouts. `bassmaster-top-lures-st-lawrence-2024.md`, `bassmaster-tn-river-classic-preview-2026.md`.
- **TVA-largemouth power-finesse — 5–7" magnum worm or stickbait.** Bass Pro Magnum Fin-Eke (DeFoe southern), Strike King Filler Worm 5" (Pzrekurat Wheeler 2024), Big Bite Scentsation Worm (Pirch Chickamauga 2022 Texas-rigged), Googan Rattlin Ned Stick (Wheeler 2019 win). Off-colored / current-broken / stained TVA water context. `wired2fish-defoe-southern-drop-shot-2024.md`, `bassmaster-top-lures-wheeler-2024.md`, `bassmaster-top-lures-chickamauga-2022.md`, `mlf-wheeler-table-rock-stage-seven-2019.md`.
- **Boot-tail swimbait variant** (Strike King Rage Swimmer 2-3/4", #2 EWG, embedded nose-hook) — for current seams in TVA water and ultra-slow slack-water suspended presentations. Empirical (Bass Fishing Life Oct 2025) — single methodology-elevated source; cite as a Working-technique variant rather than a Top-3 bait. `bassfishinglife-drop-shot-rage-swimmer-2025-10.md`.

### When it excels — convergent findings

- **Year-round** in some role across TN — but the role differs by season and venue type.
- **Spring smallmouth on TN-River Classic-style hard-bottom / brushpiles / points** — drop-shot is the named smallmouth pattern, FFS-driven (`bassmaster-tn-river-classic-preview-2026.md`).
- **Spring largemouth on stained TN-River main lake** — drop-shot underperforms; ChatterBait / jerkbait / lipless dominate (`bassmaster-top-lures-tn-river-2021.md`). **Don't cast it here.**
- **Summer largemouth on TVA water (Chickamauga, Wheeler)** — minority pattern; when used, Texas-rigged 5–6" worm on heavier-wire 1/0 + 1/4 oz, near current breaks (`bassmaster-top-lures-chickamauga-2022.md`, `bassmaster-top-lures-wheeler-2024.md`).
- **Summer / early-fall southern largemouth (DeFoe template)** — straight-shank Neko-style 1/0 + magnum worm in off-colored water (`wired2fish-defoe-southern-drop-shot-2024.md`).
- **Fall smallmouth on highland reservoirs** — TWRA fall regs (5 over 15") drive smallmouth-targeted strategies; drop-shot enters the rotation. Tims Ford 6–18 ft band (`aa-fishing-tims-ford-bass-report.md` Tier C).
- **Winter smallmouth on highland reservoirs** — Tims Ford 20–50 ft drop-shot listed; Dale Hollow guide documents suspended fish to 50 ft (`wired2fish-dale-hollow-truth-2025.md`, `aa-fishing-tims-ford-bass-report.md`). But on Dale Hollow specifically, **float-and-fly is the winter headline**, not drop-shot.

### Tennessee-specific notes — convergent findings

- **The TN drop-shot game is bifurcated.** Highland-reservoir smallmouth finesse (Dale Hollow, Center Hill, Norris, Tims Ford, Cherokee) uses 3–4" worm, #1–#2 nose-hook, 3/8–1/2 oz, FFS-vertical. TVA-largemouth power-finesse (Wheeler, Chickamauga, Watts Bar) uses 5–6" worm, 1/0 heavier-wire or Texas-rigged, 1/8–1/4 oz, near cover or current breaks. **Two sub-rigs share a name but very little gear.** This is the core dossier insight — calls for the conventional-wisdom-vs-recent-tournament split format per `decisions.md` 2026-04-26.
- **TVA current effects.** DeFoe 2019: bass on TVA-current waters hold "behind rocks, brush, logs or some other type of structure that breaks the current" (`mossyoak-defoe-river-bass-2019.md`). Drop-shot's natural application is in those slack-water pockets, not in heavy current. Strader-Walker-Morgan TVA-current-specific drop-shot specifics not located in this pass (`[THIN]` gap; `pro-roster.md` confirms this is genuinely under-covered).
- **Watts Bar context.** TVA killed hydrilla in the 1990s and grass is "finally starting to come back" — explains why TVA-largemouth water on Watts Bar / Chickamauga is more open-rock-and-channel than grass-heavy, fitting drop-shot's structure profile (`mlf-strader-watts-bar-profile.md`).
- **Cherokee FFS-vertical niche** is co-occupied by drop-shot, Damiki/jighead-minnow, and small jigheads. Drop-shot wins some events (Loftus 2nd Apr 2026) and others go to jighead-minnow (Clevenger BFL Oct 2024). `bassmaster-reagan-cherokee-2026.md`, `basscast-clevenger-cherokee-bfl-2024.md`.

### Working technique — convergent findings

- **Cadence.** Conventional shake-shake-pause is being challenged by the "do less / slow drag" school. Bass Fishing Life Aug 2025 controlled comparison shows slow rod-sweep drag with weight-on-bottom and tight-ish line is highly effective — anglers consistently overwork drop-shots; ambient water + arm motion provides plenty of action even when the angler tries to hold still. Slack-line moments after a light twitch are themselves a strike trigger. `bassfishinglife-drop-shot-retrieve-2025-08.md`, `derekkira-drop-shot-schooling-coldfront-2020-03.md`.
- **Hookset.** Sweep / reel-set on spinning gear (consensus). Cody Meyer "Bubba Shot" reel-set on baitcaster (`wired2fish-meyer-bubba-shot-2021.md`).
- **Boat positioning.** Vertical for FFS / suspended fish; long cast and drag for shore / structure work; the cast-and-drag application is where leader length matters most (long leader keeps bait above snags / vegetation per `bassfishinglife-drop-shot-leader-length-2025-05.md`).

### @bassniper / methodology-elevated empirical findings

The pre-seeded 2020-03 transcript (`bassniper-drop-shot-length-weight-bait-2020-03.md`) is the single confirmed @bassniper drop-shot source. Findings:
- Bait is more dynamic than the textbook describes — rises and falls with rod movement, slow Senko-like fall when paused.
- Wacky-rigged worm lays horizontally on a vertical drop-shot (baitfish profile), legitimate vertical / FFS use case.
- Heavier weight pulls bait closer to bottom AND adds erratic recoil action — not just for "lightest possible."
- Floating worm + 6"–8" leader holds bait perfectly suspended at fish eye-line; 4-ft leader = Carolina-rig-style suspended-fish height-keying.

The Bass Fishing Life (Steve Rogers) findings (methodology-elevated to Tier A per `decisions.md` 2026-04-26 @bassniper-weighting):
- Leader length is irrelevant for true vertical-suspended fish; matters significantly when casting and dragging (long leader keeps bait above snags / above vegetation). `bassfishinglife-drop-shot-leader-length-2025-05.md`.
- Slow drag with bottom contact is highly effective — anglers overwork drop-shots. `bassfishinglife-drop-shot-retrieve-2025-08.md`.
- 2-3/4" boot-tail swimbait on drop-shot is a legitimate current-seam and slack-water variant (the rig holds depth while current alone makes the tail swim). `bassfishinglife-drop-shot-rage-swimmer-2025-10.md`.
