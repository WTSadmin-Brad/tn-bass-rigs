## Extracted findings

- **Format:** Underwater observational footage of smallmouth bass interacting with a drop-shot bait in clear-water pre-spawn conditions on the West Coast. Not a controlled comparison.
- **Behavioral observations:** Bass position, interact with the bait, and "sometimes they suck it all the way in, other times they don't." The point of the footage is to teach anglers what bass actually do when they encounter the bait — they don't always commit, and the swallow-vs-spit decision is highly variable even with finesse presentations.
- **Why it matters:** When smallmouth "buckle down or get on the beds, nothing is gonna beat that spinning rod and that light line." Tim Little frames the drop shot as a near-required tool when the bite tightens up on smallmouth and spotted bass — relevant for highland TN reservoirs where smallmouth and Kentucky spots dominate (Dale Hollow, Center Hill, Norris, Tims Ford).
- **Limitations of the source:** No controlled variable, no side-by-side. Useful for behavioral framing and as a confirmation that the rig draws fish in clear water; not citable as primary evidence on rigging or component specifics.
