# Dylan Nutt Dominates to Claim 2026 Bassmaster Classic Title

## Extracted findings
- Winner Dylan Nutt (B.A.S.S. Nation, Nashville TN) caught largemouth and smallmouth on **a soft-plastic minnow on forward-facing sonar** as his primary winning technique across all 3 days.
- Exact gear as stated: **Berkley prototype soft-plastic minnow** (no size/color given) rigged on a **3/16-oz jighead** (brand/model not stated), fished on a **6'10" medium-light Abu Garcia Fantasista X spinning rod**, **Abu Garcia Zenon X spinning reel**, **8-lb Berkley X5 braid** main line to a **15-lb Berkley GinClear fluorocarbon leader**.
- Depth range fished: 1.5 ft to 8 ft; pattern progressed through the week; smallmouth on pea-gravel banks, largemouth in a flatter back-of-creek staging area with shoals.
- Runner-up Trey McKinney: targeted fish in groups/clusters on hard clay or sand breaks along main-lake banks with current, "deepest I caught one today was probably 8 or 9 feet, but they could be over 20. They could be over 30." Baits varied: 2.7" 6th Sense Divine swimbait, prototype 6th Sense jig, and "a minnow-style bait" (unspecified).
- 3rd place Yui Aoki (FFS specialist): **Dstyle 4-inch minnow** and **Dstyle 4-inch Geelacanth soft-plastic**, fished on current-swept structure, key depth 5–10 ft, described needing to approach "very, very slowly" to avoid spooking fish.

## Notes
- All three top finishers leaned on a jighead-minnow-on-FFS presentation or its immediate cousin (swimbait/glide) — strong 2026, TN-anchored, top-tier-pro corroboration for the braid-to-fluoro-leader spinning setup (8 lb braid / 15 lb leader) as the current standard rigging, independent of DeFoe's Bass Pro-sponsored gear.
- Venue is a current-driven river system (Ft Loudoun/Tellico), not a still highland pool like Center Hill at Hurricane Bridge — depth numbers (1.5–8 ft) are NOT directly transferable to Center Hill's mid-lake suspended-over-25-ft-channel scenario; cite this source for **line/leader/rod-class specs**, not for depth.
- Aoki's "approach very slowly, stealth" note is a transferable principle for calm, clear, low-water conditions like Center Hill Saturday.
