# Wheeler Wins Bassmaster Elite at Cherokee Lake

**URL:** https://www.bassmaster.com/news/wheeler-rolls-in-to-win/
**Author:** Bryan Brasher
**Publication:** Bassmaster.com
**Date:** February 14, 2017
**Tournament:** 2017 Bassmaster Elite at Cherokee Lake (Knoxville/Jefferson County, Tennessee)
**Fetched:** 2026-04-27
**Tier:** A (tournament results), [STALE], [TN-LOCAL]

## Extracted findings

**Winner:** Jacob Wheeler (Indianapolis, Ind.) — 69 lb 13 oz over four days; $100,000.
**Runners-up:** Jamie Hartman (69 lb 3 oz, 2nd); Jesse Wiggins (69 lb, 3rd).
**Final-day catch:** 18 lb 3 oz.

**Wheeler's winning pattern:**
- Targeted smallmouth in 20–30 feet on graphed structure.
- Primary rig: VMC Moon Eye jighead + 3-inch pearl-white soft jerkbait, dropped vertically to fish identified on a Lowrance depthfinder.
- Quote: "I could run to every rock, every boulder, every piece of structure I had found in practice."
