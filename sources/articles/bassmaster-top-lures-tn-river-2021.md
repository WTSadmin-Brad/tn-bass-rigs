## Notes

- Tier A negative evidence + `[STALE]` (March 2021) + `[TN-LOCAL]` (TN River, Knoxville Elite — Fort Loudoun/Tellico).
- **Dossier-actionable:** drop-shot is NOT the dominant pattern on the TN River main lake in early spring under prespawn-staging conditions for largemouth-dominant venues. Power and bladed bait techniques win. This is the right framing for the dossier's "Reservoirs where it underperforms" / "When not to throw it" section — drop-shot is a smallmouth/clearwater/finesse rig and is genuinely outperformed by ChatterBait/jerkbait on stained TVA largemouth water in March.
- Counterpart to `bassmaster-tn-river-classic-preview-2026.md` which does call out drop-shot for smallmouth on the same water but specifically tied to FFS over hard-bottom — different fish, different time of day, different tooling era.
- 2021 predates FFS dominance, which may partially explain the absence; but the larger dynamic (largemouth-stained-current = power baits) is independent of the FFS era.
