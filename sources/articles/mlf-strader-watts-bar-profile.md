## Extracted findings

**TN-anchor / setting:**
- Watts Bar, TN — "the reservoir above Lake Chickamauga on the Tennessee River." Strader grew up fishing it; it is his home lake.
- Brandon Coulter (Knoxville TN, on the pro-roster) hosted the night-before dinner at his house on Watts Bar — direct evidence the Strader/Coulter Knoxville-area TN-pro network is real, not just a roster artifact.

**TVA / habitat history (Strader, direct):**
- "The Tennessee Valley Authority killed off a lot of the hydrilla in the 1990s, causing the fishing to take a serious hit." Strader recalls swimming as a teenager and watching TVA dump grass-killing chemicals into the water.
- Almost 30 years later (i.e., late 2010s framing in the article), Strader says "Watts Bar is finally starting to come back as one of the best fishing lakes in the state."
- Strader prediction quote: "I don't think, I *know* that Watts Bar Lake is about to be the new Lake Chickamauga in about five years. The combination of the grass coming back that was killed off 30 years ago, plus the influx of Florida strain largemouths will make Watts Bar a go-to lake in Tennessee."

**What Strader actually threw on this morning (NOT drop-shot):**
- Zorro Buzzbait + Zoom Horny Toad trailer (early morning, ~7:20 a.m.).
- Vibrating jig (mid-morning) — caught a drum.
- PH Custom Lures P1 crankbait (chasing schooling shad on the surface).
- 4¼-inch green pumpkin Zoom Salty Super Tube — pitched between submerged dock planks. Quote: "Docks are great this time of year because they provide shade for the minnows and the bass just love to hang out there."
