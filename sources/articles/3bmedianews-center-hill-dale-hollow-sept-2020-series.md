## Extracted findings — CENTER HILL section only

### Sept 3, 2020
- Reservoir conditions: fishing GOOD, water temp 83°F, lake FALLING.
- Bass: several caught on drop shots and topwater baits while fish schooling under shad. No depth stated.
- Walleye: few caught trolling nightcrawlers on flats, 26 ft.

### Sept 10, 2020
- Reservoir conditions: fishing SLOW, water temp 82°F, lake FALLING.
- Bass: several caught AT NIGHT on soft plastics and jigs on channel banks, 20-25 ft.
- Walleye: few caught trolling nightcrawlers on flats, 25 ft.
- Catfish: flatheads on noodles/bluegill, ~15 ft deep.

### Sept 17, 2020 (this is the Brad-supplied lead)
- Reservoir conditions: fishing FAIR, water temp 82°F, lake FALLING.
- Bass: few caught on football jigs on points, 20-30 ft, main lake. Also few caught on topwater baits in the creeks while fish schooling under shad.
- Walleye: few near Floating Mill, trolling at night on flats, 25 ft.

### Sept 24, 2020
- Reservoir conditions: fishing SLOW, water temp 78°F, lake FALLING.
- Bass: few caught on topwater baits while fish chasing shad. No depth stated.
- Crappie: few on minnows around docks, 15 ft.
- Walleye: few trolling nightcrawler rigs on flats, 25 ft.
