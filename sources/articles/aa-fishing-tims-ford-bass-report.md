## Extracted findings

**Tims Ford smallmouth (TN highland reservoir, Middle TN, Franklin/Moore/Coffee counties):**
- Fall (6–18 ft): rocky shorelines; "deep-diving crankbaits…along with tube baits and drop-shot rigs. Tube jigs are a great choice when the bass are around 15 feet deep."
- Winter (20–50 ft): suspending in open water near structure. "Jigging spoons, tube baits, drop-shot worms, jigs and Ned rigs tend to tempt smallmouth in deep water."
- Spring (3–18 ft): gravel flats and rocky areas; jerkbaits, crankbaits, tubes, crayfish imitators.
- Summer (15–35 ft): early/late shallow feeding; midday on points and ledges around 20 ft.

**Tims Ford largemouth:** documented for context but drop-shot is not in the largemouth tackle list at any season.
