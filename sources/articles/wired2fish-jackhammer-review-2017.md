## Extracted findings
- **Design behavior**: 3/8 oz Jack Hammer does not rise or blow out even at max retrieve speed on a 7.1:1 reel — thin blade cuts water (low resistance) + low-center-of-gravity, flat-bottom head keeps it tracking in the strike zone regardless of speed.
- **Immediate start**: begins vibrating the instant the reel engages — relevant for short-range pitches/skips to cover, not for the long-cast slow-roll use case here.
- **Trailer keeper**: double-wire keeper design "legit" — securely holds a Z-Man Razor ShadZ trailer through 20+ fish with no glue needed.
- **Hook**: "best ChatterBait hook so far" per reviewer — good penetration on long casts, doesn't bend/flex on close-range hooksets; no bend/loss across testing.
- **Skirt-off finesse mod**: in clear water, removing the skirt entirely (fishing bare blade+trailer) produced bites and let the bait slide through grass better — a clear-water finesse variant, not directly tested against rock.
