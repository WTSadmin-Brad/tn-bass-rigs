## Extracted findings
- **Football jig (winner Jacob Wheeler + 3 others):** Wheeler won throwing a **3/4-oz Jewel football head jig with a CrushCity Cleanup Craw trailer, both in green pumpkin shades**. Third place Brent Ehrler, sixth place Adrian Avena, and eighth place Spencer Shuffield also credited a football jig for their finishes (brands/models not given for those three in this excerpt). Article frames football jig with craw trailer as the natural follow-up once crankbait/Carolina rig water has been picked over.
- **Carolina rig (5 of top 10, incl. 5th place John Hunter):** Hunter's exact rig: **3/4-oz Googan Tungsten weight + green pumpkin Googan Baits Dart**. His stated location logic: "hard-bottom areas offshore – places where the current hits and gives them a place to feed."
- **Deep crankbait (7 of top 10, incl. 7th place Cole Floyd):** ~90% of Floyd's fish came on a **chartreuse and blue back Strike King 6XD**, fished on drops 10-16 ft deep.
- **Jig-and-minnow / forward-facing sonar (4 of top 10 incl. Wheeler):** Wheeler also threw a **5.4" green shad Rapala CrushCity Freeloader on a 5/16-oz VMC Hybrid Swimbait head** ("new size... out at ICAST" per Wheeler — [SPONSOR-FLAG] new product mention).
- **Drop shot (4 of top 10 incl. 3rd place Brent Ehrler):** Ehrler: "a drop-shot with a morning dawn 6-inch Roboworm Straight Tail Worm on a 1/0 Roboworm Rebarb hook and a 1/4-oz Ark Tungsten drop-shot weight," used to slow down after reaction baits.
- **General location pattern (Michael Neal, echoed by "nearly all" top 10):** "places where the schools were set up... points, bends and on straight bars," fish found idling/graphing in practice, best depths 12-14 ft for Neal specifically (other pros' depths not given in this excerpt).
