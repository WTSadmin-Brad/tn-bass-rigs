## Extracted findings

### Brandon Palaniuk
- Hook: VMC drop-shot hook, No. 1 — nose-hooked from bottom out through the nose (not top)
- Weight: 3/8-oz Eco Pro tungsten, positioned 10–12 inches below hook
- Mainline: 8-lb Berkley FireLine braid
- Leader: 8-lb or 6-lb Trilene fluorocarbon, 10-foot leader
- Bait: Berkley PowerBait Twitch Tail Minnow (green pumpkin), soaked in Berkley Gulp Alive Marinade (shad scent)
- Quote: "It's just something I learned by trial and error, after many, many years of drop-shotting."
- Context: 2nd-place finish at Lake St. Clair; 8th place with 30 lb 10 oz after two days at Sturgeon Bay.

### Justin Lucas
- Setup: braid mainline + fluorocarbon leader
- Knot: Alberto knot (preferred over uni-to-uni connection)
- Color philosophy: green pumpkin (cloudy), black (dark), natural/watermelon/light shad (sunny); Florida — black-and-blue (morning), green pumpkin (sunny)
- Quote: "You only need two or three colors, and that works all over the country."

### Skeet Reese
- Mainline: Berkley Nanofil (uni-filament, not braided) — 10-lb test with 4–8 ft of 6-lb fluorocarbon leader, OR 12-lb Nanofil with 8-lb fluoro leader
- Knot: quadruple overhand (surgeon's knot with four wraps instead of two)
- Quote on the knot: "I've never broken off a fish at that knot."
- Rod: parabolic action / lighter-action design for aggressive hooksets
- Quote on rod: "You have to crack them to get a hook in them. But if you get bit and come out of your shoes setting the hook, like I do, you need a lighter action rod so you don't snap your line."
