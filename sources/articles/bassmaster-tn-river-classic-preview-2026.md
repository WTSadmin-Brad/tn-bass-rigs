## Extracted findings

**Conditions referenced:**
- Water temperatures "warming"; fish positioned in "prespawn staging areas"; water levels "below summer pool."
- Recent pre-practice catches: 10-pound largemouth observed; historical 8+ pound largemouth documented in March 2025 tournaments on this water.

**Smallmouth pattern called out:**
- "Drop shots and dice-style baits" — alongside jerkbaits, Ned rigs, and soft-plastic minnows.
- Target structure: "brushpiles, points and hard-bottom" locations.
- **Forward-facing sonar (FFS) is the primary location tool** for the smallmouth pattern.

**Largemouth pattern called out:**
- Shallow-water presentations — "shallow points, humps, wood and rock" with "crankbaits, jigs, vibrating jigs and jerkbaits." Drop-shot is not in the largemouth callout list.

**Historical context:**
- Jeff Gustafson's 2021 Elite winning tactic at this water referenced as a jighead-minnow precedent — relevant lineage for the 2026 FFS finesse-vertical patterns including drop-shot.
