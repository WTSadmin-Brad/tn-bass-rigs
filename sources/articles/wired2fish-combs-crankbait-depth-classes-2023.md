## Extracted findings
- **Strike King 2.5 (KVD) squarebill**: shallow workhorse, 4-5 ft around cover, up to ~7 ft on a fast retrieve in open water.
- **Strike King 5XD**: "compact," versatile mid-depth crank; reaches **12-14 ft on 15 lb line**. Good in clear water but usable in stained water too.
- **Strike King 6XD**: deep-diving; casts far due to internal weighting system; gets to bottom fast and stays there; strong deflection off cover for power cranking. Combs uses it when bass are in the **12-18 ft zone, 19 ft typical max running depth**.
