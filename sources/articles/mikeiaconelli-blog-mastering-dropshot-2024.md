## Extracted findings

### Leader length — Iaconelli's framework
- Baseline: "10 to 12 inches is average" for drop-shot leader length.
- Short range: 2–6 inches.
- Long range: 20–24 inches.

### Variation rules
- **Water clarity:** "the more stained the water, the shorter the leader." Ultra-clear water → maximize distance between weight and bait. Standard lightly-stained water → 10–12 inches.
- **Cover type:**
  - Short/low cover (gravel, new grass): short leader, keep bait close to bottom.
  - Mid-range cover (rocks, cut stumps): standard 10–12 inches.
  - Large cover (boulders, milfoil 2–3 ft tall, cane): longer leader to keep bait above cover.
- **Water temperature:**
  - Colder months: shorter leaders (fish bottom-oriented).
  - Summer heat: longer leaders (fish suspended).
  - Spring/fall: mid-range.
- **Spawning fish:** short leader excels to aggravate non-feeding spawners.
