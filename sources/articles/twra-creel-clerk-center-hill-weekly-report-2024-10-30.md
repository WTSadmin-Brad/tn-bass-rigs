## Extracted findings (verbatim)
"Center Hill 10-30-24 Forecast Contributor – Will Schibig, Region III Creel Clerk. Reservoir Conditions- Center Hill Dam is generating, on average, 1,400 cfs per day. The reservoir elevation is sitting at 630'. Surface water temperatures are in the high 60s. Water clarity in the backs of creek arms is murkier, 5-7' of visibility. Main lake visibility is clear, 10-15' of visibility. I'm seeing signs of fall turnover in major creek arms on the mid-lake, which could explain the dip in fishing productivity. Bass- Fishing is slow. The topwater bite has been poor the last few weeks with most people catching their fish off the bank in 5-15 FOW. Cranking creek channel banks in 10 FOW has been producing bites, as well as fishing slow with finesse presentations off the bank as well. There are bass schooling with bait balls offshore, but it will continue to get better as the water temperatures drop. Crappie- Fishing is descent. Crappie are being caught off tree-tops in 10-20 FOW on creek channel banks with jigs and minnows... Sunfish- Fishing is good. Big redear sunfish are being caught on main lake gravel banks in 5-10 FOW with nightcrawlers rigged on drop-shot."

## Notes
- Turnover anchor: signs of turnover in mid-lake creek arms at the END of October 2024 with surface temps in the high 60s. Mid-September 2026 with aggregator estimates in the 70s and no cold front is therefore pre-turnover [INFERRED].
- Elevation 630 ft on 2024-10-30 vs. 633.6 ft forecast for 2026-09-12: the 2026 tournament pool is within ~3.6 ft of the late-October 2024 pool — low-water bank structure the clerk describes (fish off the bank 5–15 FOW, creek-channel banks) is at similar exposure.
