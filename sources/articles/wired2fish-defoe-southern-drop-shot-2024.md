## Extracted findings

**Target species & season:** largemouth bass, summer and early fall, southern off-colored water — explicitly contrasted by DeFoe with the northern smallmouth drop-shot game.

**Tackle (all Bass Pro / Bass Pro family — DeFoe is a Bass Pro Shops pro):**
- **Worm:** Bass Pro Shops Magnum Fin-Eke Worm, Red Bug color.
- **Hook:** VMC RedLine Finesse Neko Hook, 1/0, straight-shank finesse style.
- **Weight:** tungsten drop-shot weight (size not specified by source).
- **Rod:** Bass Pro Shops Johnny Morris Platinum Signature, 7'1", Medium power, spinning.
- **Reel:** Bass Pro Shops Johnny Morris Platinum Signature spinning, size 2000.
- **Mainline:** Bass Pro Shops XPS SS Braid, 10-lb test.
- **Leader:** Bass Pro Shops XPS KVD Signature 100% Fluorocarbon, 10-lb test.

**Key framing quote (paraphrase, source notes no direct DeFoe quotes in piece):**
- "A medium action provides plenty of backbone while maintaining finesse."
- DeFoe emphasizes using longer plastic worms for summer southern bass compared to northern presentations.
