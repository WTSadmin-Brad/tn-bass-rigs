## Notes

- Tier A. Northern (Great Lakes) smallmouth water, not TN. Included because:
  1. 7 of top-10 finishing on drop-shot is the strongest single-tournament evidence in the corpus that drop-shot is the dominant smallmouth deep/clearwater rig.
  2. Brand convergence is the actionable signal: hooks are VMC RedLine, Gamakatsu G-Finesse, or Berkley Fusion19, all in the #1–#2 size band (one #6 outlier on a 3" bait); weights are 3/8 or 1/2 oz tungsten, no exceptions.
  3. Bait profile convergence: all are 3–4 inch finesse worms or shad-shaped minnow imitations.
- For TN dossier use: smallmouth on highland reservoirs (Dale Hollow, Center Hill, Norris, Tims Ford) face similar deep clearwater conditions to the St. Lawrence. The brand/size convergence is transferable to those reservoirs with `[TN-LOCAL]` corroboration; for TVA-current largemouth water (Chickamauga, Watts Bar) the same gear converges differently — see `bassmaster-top-lures-wheeler-2024.md` and `bassmaster-top-lures-chickamauga-2022.md` for the TVA-side picture (same hook sizes, lighter 1/4-oz weights, larger 5–6" worms).
- Patrick Walters #6 hook on a 3" Zoom Shimmer Shad is a specific small-bait outlier — relevant for matching small-profile prey (dice-style adjacent).
