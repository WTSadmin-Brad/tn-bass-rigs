## Extracted findings

**Tournament:** Phoenix Bass Fishing League Wild Card, Cherokee Lake (TN), late October 2024.
**Winner:** Gavin Clevenger (Knoxville, TN) — 10 bass, 26 lb 14 oz; $2,833 + 2025 All-American qualification.

**Winning technique (NOT drop-shot):**
- Garmin LiveScope to identify keeper smallmouth.
- Queen Tackle 3/16- or 1/4-oz jigheads.
- Rotated through multiple areas; reacted to daily fish movement.

**TWRA seasonal regulation note:** Fall limits allow five smallmouth over 15" (versus summer's one over 18"), letting Clevenger target smallmouth aggressively in fall.

**Conditions:** Fish had shifted shallower due to reduced current and cooler weather vs. September practice.

**Direct quote:** "I stayed in those areas and found some stuff that people weren't really fishing as much as the obvious stuff."
