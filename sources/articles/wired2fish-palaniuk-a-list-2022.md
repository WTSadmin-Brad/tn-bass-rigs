## Extracted findings

Palaniuk uses a single base rod/reel/mainline for all drop-shot setups; what changes is the leader, hook, weight, and bait by application.

### Setup 1 — Nose-hooked plastic (open-water finesse default)
- **Hook:** **VMC Drop Shot Hook, Size 2** (open hook for nose-hooking).
- **Weight:** **VMC Tungsten Teardrop Drop Shot Weight, 1/8–3/4 oz** (range covers depths and conditions).
- **Mainline:** Seaguar Smackdown Braided, 15-lb (Flash Green).
- **Leader:** **Seaguar Gold Label Fluorocarbon, 6–12 lb.**
- **Rod:** Alpha Angler DSR Spinning, 6'10" Medium.
- **Reel:** Daiwa Exist 3000 Spinning.
- **Bait:** X-Zone 4" Original Slammer (nose-hooked).

### Setup 2 — Threaded plastic (cover/weedless)
- **Hook:** **VMC Finesse Neko Hook, Size 2** (Texas/threaded rigging for cover and larger profiles).
- **Weight:** Same — VMC Tungsten Teardrop, 1/8–3/4 oz.
- **Mainline:** Same — Seaguar Smackdown 15-lb.
- **Leader:** **Seaguar Tatsu Fluorocarbon, 6–12 lb** (Tatsu is the premium-grade leader he uses with the threaded setup; Gold Label on Setup 1).
- **Rod:** Same — Alpha Angler DSR 6'10" M Spinning.
- **Reel:** Same — Daiwa Exist 3000.
- **Bait:** X-Zone 6" MB Fat Finesse Worm (threaded Texas-style).

### Quote
- "A drop shot can be fished shallow to deep, in open water or around cover, and in clear or dingy water."

### Weight-shape philosophy (from search-snippet corroboration of the same A-List content)
- Palaniuk's stated reasoning for **VMC Tungsten Teardrop**: "best feel and performs better than ball shapes that tend to hang up more or cylinder styles that create line twist." (Note: Wheeler favors VMC Cylinder for the opposite reason — cover/timber penetration. Worth surfacing as a `[CONTESTED]` weight-shape callout in the dossier.)

The Wheeler 7'2" MH is the cover-fishing variant; the Palaniuk 6'10" M is the open-water default. Both legitimate; pair with bait/cover application.
