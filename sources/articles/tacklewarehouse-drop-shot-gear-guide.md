## Extracted findings

### Hooks
- **Brands & models:** Hayabusa DSR132, VMC Weedless Neko, Eagle Claw Lazer Sharp Drop Shot, Gamakatsu Drop/Split Shot, BKK Armor Point Drop Shot Worm, Owner Jungle Drop Shot
- **Size range:** #2 to #1/0 drop-shot hooks; #1 to #1/0 worm hooks depending on bait type
- **Rigging methods:** nose-hooking with open-style hooks for maximum tail action; Texas-style rigging on straight-shank/rebarb hooks for weedy areas; wacky rigging in warmer months

### Weights
- **Starting size:** 3/16 oz, adjusting up for wind and depth
- **Material/shape:** standard drop-shot weights (specific shape not detailed in fetch)

### Line setup
- **Mainline:** 12–15 lb braid (high-vis recommended for strike indication)
- **Leader:** 6–10 lb fluorocarbon (8 lb suggested as starting point)
- **Configuration:** "Braided mainline attached to a fluorocarbon leader" improves sensitivity and reduces line twist

### Knots
- **Recommended:** Palomar — "incredibly quick, easy to tie"

### Rod
- **Length:** 6'10" to 7'3"
- **Power:** Medium-light to medium
- **Action:** Fast or extra-fast tip for quick hooksets
- **Quote:** "Sensitivity is king"

### Reel
- **Size:** 2500–3000 (some anglers prefer 4000-size for reduced line twist)
- **Type:** Spinning with smooth-starting drag

### Top drop-shot baits
- **Worms:** Roboworm Fat Straight Tail, Straight Tail, Alive Shad; Zoom Tiny Fluke, Finesse Worm; Berkley PowerBait MaxScent Flat Worm; Yamamoto 3" Shad Shape, Kut Tail; Strike King KVD Dream Shot
- **Other plastics:** swimbaits, minnows, shad-shaped lures, finesse creature baits, tubes
- **Color strategy:** natural greens/browns for clear water; vibrant accents (chartreuse tails) for dirty water/low light

### Notable pro quote
- **Aaron Martens:** drop shot is "the single best tactic for catching bass all throughout the country."
