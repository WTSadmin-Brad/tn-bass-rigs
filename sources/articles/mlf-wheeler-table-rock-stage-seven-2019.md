## Extracted findings

### Drop-shot setup (Wheeler's primary final-day producer)

- **Bait:** Hand-poured straight-tail worm AND a Googan Baits Drag n Drop, both in **Morning Dawn** color.
- **Hook:** **1/0 VMC Finesse Neko Hook**.
- **Rigging:** Texas-rigged, NOT nose-hooked.
  - Wheeler quote: "I was dropping it into brush and timber, and a nose-hooked bait hangs up more. Plus, I think you hook them better with a Texas-rig."
- **Weight:** **1/4-oz VMC Cylinder Drop-Shot weight.**
- **Rod:** **7'2" Duckett Jacob Wheeler Signature Series spinning rod, medium-heavy.**
- **Reel:** 3000-size spinning reel.
- **Mainline:** **8-lb Sufix NanoBraid.**
- **Leader:** **10-lb Sufix Advance Fluorocarbon.** "due to the heavy cover he was fishing."

### Working pattern context
- Targeted points, humps, submerged trees, offshore locales; later keyed on docks.
- "My first cast to each area was with the swimbait to try to catch the easy ones if they were grouped up. Then, I switched to the drop-shot and could pick off individual fish."
