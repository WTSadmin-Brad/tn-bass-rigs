# 5 Must-Know Drop Shot Tactics

**Fetched:** 2026-04-27

## Extracted findings

### 1. Locked-down bass / deadsticking — Aaron Martens
- Bait: 4.5-inch Roboworm Straight-Tail Worm, nose-hooked
- Hook: No. 1 Gamakatsu G-Finesse Drop Shot Hook
- Drop line (leader): 4–10 inches in spring; 15–20 inches in fall
- Quote: "It's one of the hardest things to do. It's like slamming on the brakes."

### 2. Suspended bass — Kotaro Kiriyama
- Lake Erie setup: 1/4-oz sinker 12" above hook; Jackall Crosstail Shad nose-hooked; No. 2 Owner Mosquito Hook
- Lake Hartwell setup: 1/8-oz weight; small shad imitation Texas-rigged; 1/0 Owner J Light Hook
- Quote: "It was like playing Super Nintendo. I would see the bass on my graph and let the drop shot sink straight down."

### 3. Swift current — Kevin VanDam
- Leader: 8-lb fluorocarbon
- Hook: No. 2 Mustad Double Wide KVD Drop Shot Hook (12 inches above weight)
- Weight: 3/16 or 1/4 oz; deep water 3/8 to 1/2 oz
- Bait: Strike King Drop Shot Half Shell, nose-hooked
- Quote: "If the weight was too heavy, it would hang on the bottom and kill the bait's action."

### 4. Shallow cover — Justin Lucas
- Rod/reel: spinning outfit (specs not given)
- Mainline: 10-lb Berkley FireLine
- Leader: 10-lb Berkley 100% Fluorocarbon
- Weight: 3/16-oz (8 inches below hook)
- Hook: 1/0 straight-shank
- Bait: 6-inch hand-poured worm, or 6.25-inch Berkley Havoc Bottom Hopper
- Quote: "With a baitcaster, I have to pull line off the spool to get a vertical drop."

### 5. Drop shot drag — Brent Ehrler
- Rod/reel: spinning
- Mainline: 12-lb Sunline braid
- Leader: 8-lb Sunline FC Sniper, 10-foot leader
- Weight: 1/4-oz tungsten (14 inches above hook)
- Hook: No. 1 Gamakatsu Drop/Split Shot Hook
- Bait: 4-inch or 6-inch Roboworm, nose-hooked
- Quote: "They'll hit the drop shot when I drag it, but they won't when I shake it on the bottom."
