## Extracted findings

DeFoe's river-fishing philosophy. No drop-shot specifics in this piece — included for TN-anchored context on current and structure.

**Quotes:**
- "My definition of a river fisherman is an angler who loves to fish current (moving water) for bass."
- "The bass won't be out in the middle of the heavy current. They'll be behind rocks, brush, logs or some other type of structure that breaks the current."
- "Generally, this structure is so close to the current that when the bass see bait in the current, they can dart out from behind the slack-water barriers where they're holding and eat that bait."
- "I know when I locate those kinds of places in a river that if I cast my bait to pass in front of the bass, then if a bass is holding there, it will take my bait."
