## Extracted findings
- **Strike King HC KVD Splash Popper**, 2 1/4 in, 3/8 oz — author's go-to for schooling bass busting shad on the surface; the bait's "subtle spitting sound" is described as closely mimicking a shad breaking the surface. Author notes a long-held assumption that walking baits are the default schooler bait was wrong in his own experience — a popper "wrecked them" when walking baits and soft jerkbaits got zero looks on the same school. Best fished by casting to the baitball itself, not covering water.
- **Blue Fox Flash Spinner**, inline spinner, 3/16 oz, 30° blade angle — designed to run 5–6 ft deep, under the baitball, for bass that are positioned below the visible surface feed rather than actively busting; cast past the ball, let fall on slack line, then lightly pop the rod tip.
- **Yo-Zuri Rattl'n Vibe**, lipless crankbait — used to cover water fast across flats/ditches in search mode; cast past the ball, let it sink, retrieve into the bait and pause/fall through it (bites come on the fall).
- General principle: fish AROUND/PAST the baitball and let baits fall through it rather than casting into the thick of the visible surface activity.
