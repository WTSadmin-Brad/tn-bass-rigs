## Notes on relevance to 2026 tournament prep

**Generation/current-position statement (item 5 target):** The clearest named, dated Center-Hill-specific statement on generation effects on bass position found in this research pass is from 2024-05-15: during a high-generation flood-recovery release (13,900 cfs sluicing), points and humps positioned bass to ambush baitfish in the current, and steeper banks (less affected by rising water) fished more consistently. This is a **high-flow flood-control event, not a normal single-unit afternoon-generation day** — scale/context differ sharply from the Saturday 2026-09-12 forecast (no generation at all during tournament hours, ~3,760 cfs one-unit afternoons on other days). Treat as a general current/ambush-point principle, not a directly transferable number.

**No 2024→2026 continuity confirmed.** This snapshot is 2+ years stale and reservoir elevation values (644–658') are far above the 2026 September range (633.6–635 ft) — a fundamentally different hydrologic regime (spring/early-summer post-flood-control vs. drought-driven fall drawdown). Cited here only for the generation-effect mechanism and as a seasonal-pattern skeleton, not as a current-conditions source.

**Coolest-water note:** The 2024-06-05 entry ("coolest water temperatures... near Rock Island, in the 60s") suggests up-lake/upriver areas historically run cooler than the main lake/dam area in early summer — worth a mental note for late-season thermal refuges, but again 2024-dated and un-confirmed for 2026.
