# pro-roster.md

The pro roster is a research net, not a vetting checklist. The job of this file is to help you cast wide across multiple credible voices, then filter for consensus. Lots of sources, then triangulate.

Per Brad's framing: sponsorship isn't disqualifying. It's contextual information that tells you when to require additional corroboration before treating a claim as universal. A pro endorsing their own sponsor's product can still be right — but the claim deserves independent confirmation, especially when the sponsorship is a primary income source. Where consensus exists across pros with *different* sponsorships, confidence rises sharply.

Most of the time, dossier specificity will live at the **bait family / material** level rather than the exact brand level. "Any premium 5-inch stickbait in green pumpkin" is often the right level of detail, not "Yamamoto Senko 5-inch #297." Specific brands matter when their action, salt content, or material is genuinely distinct (Keitech swimbaits, for example, behave differently from competitors). The pros help you figure out which case you're in. When five pros all swear by one specific product across different sponsorships, brand-level specificity is earned. When they describe the same general bait differently, family-level is the right call.

## Currency disclaimer

This roster reflects information current as of the file's last update. Sponsorships shift, anglers retire, rising names emerge. Before relying on a pro's content as a primary source, verify they're still active, still affiliated as listed, and still relevant to current circuits. Update this file as you discover changes — append findings to `decisions.md` when a sponsor change or retirement materially affects how we weight a source.

## How to use this file

When researching a rig:

1. Pull at least 4–6 pros from this roster who have credible content on that rig — mix across tiers. A drop shot dossier might draw from Cody Meyer (specialist), Jacob Wheeler (tour star), Ott DeFoe (TN-anchored), and @bassniper (empirical), then add others as their coverage surfaces.
2. Look for what they agree on. Document agreement at whatever specificity level it actually exists — brand, family, or material.
3. Where they disagree, document the conditions driving the disagreement. Don't average them.
4. Cross-reference everything against the empirical-data tier (Tier 4) before treating any claim as settled.

## Tier 1 — Triangulation core

Tour-level pros with broad relevance across many rigs. These are the first stops for most rigs. Strong content output, recognized expertise, and patterns that get picked up and tested by the rest of the bass community.

**Jacob Wheeler** — MLF Bass Pro Tour, multiple BPT Angler of the Year wins, prolific content producer. Cofounder of Googan Squad, longtime Strike King association, Lew's reels. YouTube channel with extensive technique breakdowns. Often the trend-setter for emerging techniques. Verify current sponsorships before citing brand-specific claims.

**Brandon Palaniuk** — Bassmaster Elite Series, technique-heavy content style. History of detailed gear breakdowns. Sponsorship landscape has shifted over the years — verify current affiliations. Strong on smallmouth and finesse work.

**Dustin Connell** — MLF Bass Pro Tour, multiple major wins. Less content production than Wheeler/Palaniuk, but tournament results carry weight. Useful for confirming what's actually winning vs. what's being demonstrated on YouTube.

**Jordan Lee** — Bassmaster Classic winner, MLF-active. Strong fundamentals reputation, less brand-loud than some peers.

**Bryan Thrift** — MLF/BPT veteran, Damiki and Fitzgerald associations historically. Power-fishing pedigree. Especially relevant for jigs and Carolina-rig style techniques.

**Greg Hackney** — Multi-circuit veteran, deep jig specialist. Especially relevant for the football jig dossier and any flipping/punching content.

**John Cox** — MLF, shallow-water specialist, often unconventional approaches. Useful for confirming when shallow-water rigs (Texas, free, punch) are working in unexpected conditions.

**Brandon Cobb** — Bassmaster Elite, southern circuit experience that overlaps with TN waters geographically.

## Tier 2 — TN-anchored

Pros who live, fish, or built their career on Tennessee reservoirs. Their water-specific knowledge often goes deeper than what national pros pick up during tournament weeks. For TN-specific dossier sections, these voices weigh heavier.

**Ott DeFoe** — Knoxville-based, Bassmaster Classic winner, deep TN reservoir experience including Douglas, Cherokee, Watts Bar, Tellico. Mercury Marine, Storm Lures historically. Strong on smallmouth + largemouth mixed-bag patterns that match our project's reality.

**Wesley Strader** — Spring City TN, longtime tournament veteran. Especially valuable for technique nuance and TVA-current-aware patterns. Has produced YouTube content with technique focus.

**Andy Morgan** — Dayton TN, FLW/MLF dominant career, more selectively active in recent years. Legacy reference for power-fishing techniques on TVA waters. If still actively producing content as of your research date, his TN-water knowledge is unmatched.

**David Walker** — TN-based, multi-decade tournament experience, FLW/MLF history. Solid for traditional technique baselines on TN waters.

**Brandon Coulter** — Knoxville TN, more regional/college-level visibility. Useful for catching emerging trends among younger TN-based anglers before they hit national circuits.

## Tier 3 — Technique specialists

When a rig has an obvious go-to expert, that voice carries extra weight on that specific rig. Use these for depth on the rig they're known for.

**Cody Meyer** — Drop shot. Decades of finesse-specialty content. Default first stop for any drop-shot dossier work.

**Mike Iaconelli** — Finesse and power both. "Going Ike" content channel produces detailed breakdowns. Useful across most rigs in the Phase 1 list.

**Justin Lucas** — Finesse-leaning, especially Ned-rig and shaky head territory. West Coast finesse pedigree applied to broader bass fishing.

**Kevin VanDam (KVD)** — Largely retired from competition but content and influence persist. Legacy reference especially for Texas rig and power techniques. Verify any current claim attributed to him is recent rather than archival.

## Tier 4 — Empirical content creators

Different category. These voices are weighted on the basis of their methodology rather than tournament results. When they confirm or contradict the consensus from Tiers 1–3, that's high-signal information for the dossier.

**@bassniper** — Priority empirical source. Scientific-method approach with underwater video and systematic experimentation. See `source-hierarchy.md` for full weighting rationale. Confirmations and contradictions both go in the dedicated `@bassniper empirical findings` section of every dossier.

**TacticalBassin (Matt Allen, Tim Little)** — Broad-coverage, technique-detail-heavy YouTube channel. Less explicitly experimental than @bassniper but consistently rigorous and named hosts.

**BassResource (Glenn May)** — Long-running content site with structured technique guides and community Q&A. Useful for community-consensus signals when forum patterns are hard to read.

**LakeForkGuy (Justin Rackley)** — TX-based but broad relevance, mid-range empirical bent, accessible technique content.

Add others to this tier as you encounter them. Threshold for inclusion: methodology that goes beyond demonstrating-what-the-pros-said. Underwater video, controlled comparisons, repeated trials, or systematic data collection.

## What's not in this file

The schema for *how* to cite a pro lives in `CLAUDE.md` and `schema.md`. The detailed source ranking — what counts as a credible source format and what doesn't — lives in `source-hierarchy.md`. This file is purely about who to listen to.

When sponsor relationships are discovered during research, the *fact* of the relationship can be noted inline with the claim using `[SPONSOR-FLAG]` per the schema. Detailed sponsor history doesn't need to clutter this file — keep it focused on identity and triangulation utility.

## Maintenance protocol

When you discover a sponsor change, retirement, or new technique-specialist worth adding:

1. Update the relevant pro's entry here.
2. Append a dated entry to `decisions.md` if the change materially affects existing dossiers (e.g., a Tier-1 pro retires and their content becomes archival rather than current).
3. Note the source of the update in your entry — a transcript, an article, a tournament press release.

This file gets stale faster than the schema or style guide. Treat updates as part of the research workflow, not a separate chore.
